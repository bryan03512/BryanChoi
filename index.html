Pose Trainer

Pose Trainer is a browser app that uses your webcam to learn poses you record, such as martial arts stances, yoga, dance or physical therapy moves. It then recognizes those poses live and coaches you joint by joint, and everything runs on your device without sending any video.

CheckedMath

CheckedMath is a math tutoring website I built where students describe exactly what they're stuck on and get personalized, print-ready worksheets and step-by-step explanations: AI drafts each one, a second AI pass re-solves every problem to catch mistakes, and I review the results before they're emailed out. It also offers free practice problems on 17 topics that check answers instantly, a daily challenge, and printable practice sheets, built with React, TypeScript, Supabase, the Anthropic API, and Cloudflare Turnstile for spam protection.
